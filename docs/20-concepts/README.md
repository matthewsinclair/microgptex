# 20 Concepts To Understand AI in 2026

A short, self-contained field guide to the twenty ideas that explain how modern AI works — from the single neuron up to the retrieval-augmented, agentic systems behind today's products. Twenty chapters across four parts, two to three pages each, with every claim linked to a verifiable source.

Start at the **[table of contents](000-toc.md)**.

The concept list is adapted from a thread by [@sairahul1](https://xcancel.com/sairahul1/status/2057740928908161461?s=43); the anchor reference is Stanford's [CS229 lecture on Building Large Language Models](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts). Full credits live in the [references](502-references.md).
